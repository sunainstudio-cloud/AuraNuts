About the company

What if healthy snacking could be simple enough for everyone?

AuraNuts began in Bengaluru in May 2026 with a simple belief: enjoying dry fruits shouldn't be limited to special occasions.

Founded by Syed Waseem Ul Haque, AuraNuts was created to make dry-fruit snacking more convenient, approachable, and affordable for everyday customers.
From classic dry fruits and honey-infused combinations to customized creations and thoughtful gifting, we're building a brand around choice, convenience, and the joy of sharing.
Whether it's a snack for yourself or a gift for someone special, we want AuraNuts to be part of your everyday moments and celebrations.
